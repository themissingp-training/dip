Three fixes for presentations that don't move people

Hi {{first_name}},

Most presentations fail before the first slide. Here are three fixes your team can use in their very next meeting:

—Start with the decision you need, not the background. Say it in the first 30 seconds.
—One slide, one message. If a slide needs two headlines, it needs two slides.
—End with a clear ask: who does what, by when.

These come from our two-day Delivering Impactful Presentations program, where every participant presents, gets feedback, and presents again.
