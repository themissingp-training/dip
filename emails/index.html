How it works

From Google Sheet to inbox, automatically

01

Client list

A Google Sheet with every client: name, email, company, and which email they received last.

02

Daily check

Make.com runs once a day and finds clients whose next email is due (15 days after the last).

03

Pick the email

It picks the next email in the series and fills in their name and company.

04

Send from Gmail

Sent from your real address, so replies come straight to you.

05

Update & stop

The sheet records what was sent. Anyone who replies or unsubscribes is skipped from then on.

Good practice

So emails land in the inbox, not spam

Send gently

  • Start with 20–30 emails a day, then grow
  • Send Tuesday–Thursday, 10am–12pm
  • Gmail limit: about 500/day (2,000 on Google Workspace)

Stay personal

  • Send from a real person, not "info@"
  • Always use {{first_name}} and {{company}}
  • Reply to every response yourself

Respect the inbox

  • Every email has a one-line unsubscribe
  • Stop the series when someone replies
  • After email 6, pause that client for 3 months
